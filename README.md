## 🛠️ Projects I've Shipped

### 🧠 [ORBIT AI: Personal AI Employee](https://github.com/Muhammad-Hassaan11/AI_Employee_Vault)
An autonomous agent that reads Gmail and WhatsApp, drafts replies, schedules posts and handles invoicing through self hosted Odoo. Every sensitive action goes through a human approval queue, and everything is logged in an Obsidian vault.
`Python` `Claude Code` `MCP` `Odoo` `Obsidian`

### 📚 [StudyPilot](https://github.com/Muhammad-Hassaan11/Study-Pilot)
A mobile app that scans your university timetable from a photo and builds your weekly schedule. A vision model reads the image, a Supabase Edge Function keeps API keys off the device, and every response is validated with Zod.
`React Native` `Expo` `Supabase` `Groq` `TypeScript`

### 🎯 [Prospector: Lead Generation Tool](https://github.com/Muhammad-Hassaan11/lead-gen-UI) · [Live](https://lead-gen-ui-frontend.vercel.app/)
Drop in Google Maps URLs and get enriched leads with business info, emails and social profiles, synced straight to Google Sheets. Turned a messy 58 row scrape into 55 verified leads.
`Python` `Web Scraping` `Google Sheets API`

### 🏭 [Supply Chain Management System](https://github.com/Muhammad-Hassaan11/Supply-Chain-Mangament-System) · [Live](https://supply-chain-mangament-system.vercel.app/)
